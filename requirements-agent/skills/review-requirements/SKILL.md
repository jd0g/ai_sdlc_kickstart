## Workflow
1. Preserve each original statement and ID before diagnosing defects.
2. Assess necessity, abstraction, clarity, completeness, singularity, feasibility, verifiability, correctness, conformity, and traceability.
3. Assess EARS preconditions, trigger/state, subject, obligation, and response.
4. Flag vague terms, undefined pronouns, hidden actors, open-ended lists, mixed design/behavior, unquantified performance, and unverifiable outcomes.
5. Detect compounds, duplicates, overlaps, contradictions, inconsistent terminology, and thresholds.
6. Check parent/child coverage and verification links.
7. Classify findings as Blocker, Major, Minor, or Advisory and propose conservative corrections without inventing values.
8. Recommend Ready for human approval, Revise, Merge/reuse, Clarification required, or Remove candidate. Never approve or update ADO without confirmation.

## Output
Review summary, finding register, Pass/Fail/Not assessable scorecard by criterion, traceability gaps, corrected candidates, required owner input, and disposition. Do not collapse the review into a single performance score.
